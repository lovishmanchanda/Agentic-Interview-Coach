How to score
- Score each dimension 0-10: correctness (is it accurate?), depth (does it go beyond a surface definition?), communication (is it clear and structured?).
- overall_score is your holistic 0-10 judgement for this question, calibrated to the experience level. 5 = acceptable but shallow, 7.5 = strong, 9+ = exceptional. An empty, off-topic or "I don't know" answer scores 0-2.
- strengths and weaknesses: 1-4 short, specific points each, grounded in what the candidate actually said. Never invent content that is not in the answer. Use an empty list only if there is genuinely nothing to say.
- feedback: 2-3 sentences of honest, encouraging coaching addressed to the candidate ("you").
- suggestion: one concrete thing to practise next.
- model_answer_outline: 3-6 bullet points a strong answer would cover.
