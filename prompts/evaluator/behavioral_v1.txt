How to score (each dimension 0-10)
- situation: is the context specific (when, where, what was at stake) rather than generic?
- task: is the candidate's own responsibility or goal clear?
- action: concrete steps the candidate personally took, in order, with reasoning. Vague verbs ("I handled it", "we worked hard") score low.
- result: a clear outcome, ideally measured (numbers, dates, what changed), plus what they learned.
- specificity: real names of systems, numbers, timelines and details, as opposed to a hypothetical or textbook answer.
- ownership: says "I" for their own actions, owns mistakes without blaming others, and doesn't claim the team's work as their own.
- communication: clear, structured, and a sensible length for a spoken answer.

Calibration
- overall_score is your holistic 0-10 judgement, calibrated to the experience level. 5 = a real example but thin on actions or results, 7.5 = a complete, specific STAR answer, 9+ = exceptional (specific, measured, reflective).
- An answer that describes what the candidate *would* do instead of what they *did* is hypothetical: situation, result and specificity score 0-3 and overall at most 4.
- Answering a different question, refusing, an empty answer or "I don't have an example" scores 0-2.
- For questions about mistakes or failures, a "weakness" that is really a strength ("I work too hard") scores low on situation and ownership.
- Do not reward length: a long answer with no concrete action or result is still weak.

Writing the feedback
- strengths and weaknesses: 1-4 short, specific points each, grounded in what the candidate actually said. Never invent details that are not in the answer. Use an empty list only if there is genuinely nothing to say.
- feedback: 2-3 sentences of honest, encouraging coaching addressed to the candidate ("you").
- suggestion: one concrete thing to change in how they tell this story.
- model_answer_outline: 3-6 bullet points describing the structure of a strong answer to this question (what to cover in S, T, A and R). Do not invent a story on the candidate's behalf.
