How to score (each dimension 0-10, calibrated to the experience level):

- approach: is the algorithm sound and reasonably efficient for the constraints?

- code_quality: readability, naming, structure, no dead code.
- edge_cases: empty input, single element, duplicates, boundaries, as the problem needs.
- communication: did the explanation make the approach and trade-offs clear? No explanation scores 0-2 here only.

- strengths and weaknesses: 1-4 short, specific points each, grounded in the actual code and explanation. Hidden tests are confidential: you may say how many failed, never guess their inputs.
- feedback: 2-3 sentences of honest, encouraging coaching addressed to the candidate ("you").
